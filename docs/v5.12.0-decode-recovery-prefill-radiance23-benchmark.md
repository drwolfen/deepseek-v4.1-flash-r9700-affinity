## Executive Summary

Wave 15 (`v5.12.0`) delivers a whole-engine optimization sweep targeting three goals: recovering `v5.8.0`-era decode speed, closing the prefill throughput gap against `StillDeadcode/radiance` `v2.3.0`, and hardening prefix-cache continuity plus dynamic VRAM expert lending:

1. **Prefill & Strided Throughput Leap (`+17.74%` to `+43.57%` vs `v5.11.0`, `prefill_chunk=4096`):**  
   Waves 1–4 raise **`p4k pp1` from `557.0 -> 799.7 tok/s` (`+43.57%`)**, **`l4k pp1` from `651.4 -> 922.7 tok/s` (`+41.65%`)**, **`code_edit pp1` from `783.6 -> 1,018.3 tok/s` (`+29.95%`)**, **`l16k pp1` from `1,331.9 -> 1,623.3 tok/s` (`+21.88%`)**, **`l64k pp1` from `1,425.1 -> 1,677.9 tok/s` (`+17.74%`)**, and **`l64k pp2` strided prefill from `2,657.4 -> 3,378.4 tok/s` (`+27.13%`, peaking at `3,385.0 tok/s` in `c100_g0_l1` and `3,503.6 tok/s` in `graph_0`)**.
2. **Decode Recovery Surpasses `v5.4.0` Across All Metrics and Wins `p4k`/`l4k`/`l64k`/`step_c` vs `v5.11.0`:**  
   Chart note: `56.25 tok/s` (`l64k tg2`) and `54.94 tok/s` (`l4k tg2`) in `performance-progression.svg` belonged to `v5.8.0` (`ecfb36d`), whereas `v5.4.0` recorded `46.73` (`l64k tg2`), `48.24` (`l4k tg2`), and `22.70` (`4p`). Against `v5.4.0`, `v5.12.0` (`final_v512 @ fa9267b`) improves `l64k tg2` to **`53.58 tok/s`** (**`+14.66%`**, reaching **`56.95 tok/s`** in paired `batch_b` arm `c100_g0_l1`), `l4k tg2` to **`53.00 tok/s`** (**`+9.87%`**), and `step_c` to **`25.68 tok/s`** (**`+13.13%`**). Against `v5.11.0`, `v5.12.0` improves `p4k tg2` (**`19.97` vs `18.16`**, `+9.97%`), `l4k tg2` (**`53.00` vs `51.51`**, `+2.89%`), `l64k tg2` (**`53.58` vs `50.78`**, `+5.51%`), and `step_c` (**`25.68` vs `25.04`**, `+2.56%`, **`129.02 ms/blk` vs `136.14 ms/blk`**, `-7.12 ms/blk`).
3. **Root-Caused ROCm `#9297` Queue-Count Penalty Under `AFF_HYBRID_CU_FRAC=0.25`:**  
   During `diag_regime` and `fast_matrix` bisection, setting `AFF_HYBRID_CU_FRAC=0.25` created an extra CU-masked compute stream per card (`8` total KFD queues instead of `6`), dropping `l4k tg2` from **`48.24 -> 36.71 tok/s`** (`-23.9%`) and `l64k tg2` from **`56.95 -> 39.74 tok/s`** (`-30.2%`) while degrading `step_c` to `20.60 tok/s` (`167.84 ms/blk`). Locking default `AFF_HYBRID_CU_FRAC=1.0` keeps unmasked `32/32` WGP streams (`6` KFD queues total) and recovers full decode speed.
4. **Dynamic VRAM Loan (`+108` Decode Expert Slots -> `4,027` Effective Decode Experts) & Ext-Arena Isolation (`fa9267b`):**  
   `AFF_VRAM_LOAN=1` loans unused prefill scratch tail memory (`b_qtm` / prefill workspace) during narrow decode (`328.2 MiB/card`, `6,224 KiB/slot`) to hold **`+108` hot decode expert slots** atop **`3,919` base resident experts** (`3,846` shared + `73` Card-1 bonus `443.7 MiB`), while `fa9267b` isolates decode `ext_bydn_`/`ext_ba8_` arenas and unconditionally syncs mover streams `I.mv[dv]` on reclaim.
5. **Radiance `v2.3.0` Kernel & Runtime Ports + Unframed Thinking Split Fix (`aadd6b4`):**  
   Ported `exp2` softmax / late-K FlashAttention (`AFF_FLASH_EXP2`, `AFF_FLASH_KLATE`), `nosplit` low-M FlashAttention & draft Flash (`AFF_FLASH_LOWM_NOSPLIT`, `AFF_DRAFT_FLASH`), vectorized mHC mix (`AFF_MHC_MIX_FAST`), wave-level router top-K (`AFF_ROUTER_TOPK_V2`), DPP Hadamard expert rotation (`AFF_EXPERT_HAD32_DPP`), TP all-reduce parity guards (`AFF_TP_AR_PARITY`), greedy-local head argmax (`AFF_HEAD_GREEDY_LOCAL`), piecewise `1 GiB` `hipHostRegister` copy chunking, opt-in DSpark HIP graphs (`AFF_DSPARK_HIP_GRAPH`), and `split_thinking_fallback()` for Turn-2 prefix-cache continuity when Turn 1 terminates without a closing `</think>` boundary in `content`/`reasoning_content` metadata.

## 2. Root-Cause Bisection (`diag_regime`) & `batch_b` Paired Ablation

### 2.1 `diag_regime`: Why `AFF_HYBRID_CU_FRAC=0.25` Cost `-24%` to `-30%` Decode Speed

Even with `GPU_MAX_HW_QUEUES=1`, creating a CU-masked hybrid stream via `hipExtStreamCreateWithCUMask` (`AFF_HYBRID_CU_FRAC=0.25`, `0x000000ff`) forced HIP to allocate an extra hardware KFD queue per GPU (`8` queues total vs `6` queues unmasked), triggering the ROCm `#9297` scheduling wait penalty. Synthetic `hip_micro` confirmed identical raw PCIe copy bandwidth (`DUAL 32/32 WGPs: 27.55 GB/s` vs `8/32 WGPs: 27.58 GB/s`) and microsecond queue-depth delta (`0.45 -> 0.55 us/iter` at `1` vs `4` queues), proving the regression occurred exclusively when the server's hybrid MoE streams allocated `8` live KFD queues:

## 3. Architecture & Code Changes (`4d8478c` → `fa9267b`)
