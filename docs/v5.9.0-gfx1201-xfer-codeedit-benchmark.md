## 1. Executive Summary

`v5.9.0` (`e41f0c5`, Wave 12) audits the full data-transfer path across PCIe Gen3 ×16, GPU0↔GPU1 peer copies, GDDR6 VRAM, and RDNA 4 (`gfx1201`) caches/LDS, reclaims **`~518–520 MiB/card`** of non-expert VRAM via workspace aliasing, `VirtualSpan` lazy indexer commits, and TP=2 weight sharding, and resolves the `v5.8.0` `code_edit` speculative decoding regression while preserving `-ffp-contract=off` (`33/33`), RDNA 4 zero-scratch/zero-VGPR-spill (`29,585` audited kernels across `14` ELF code objects), and persistent prefix-cache compatibility (`0x733de762fee543e6`):

- **Lossless Compression vs. Zero-Copy UVA Audit**:
  - Measuring `model-moonmath-1017.aff` confirmed Shannon entropy of **`7.996 bits/byte`**, **`0.41%` zero bytes**, **`zlib -1` ratio `1.0003`**, and **`0 B` padding** on already 2.875-bpw FP4/FP8 packed containers. Any CPU/GPU lossless compression pass over routed MoE expert streams would add latency and compute without shrinking PCIe wire volume over zero-copy UVA.
  - Instead, reclaiming VRAM to increase static GPU-resident MoE coverage removes PCIe expert streaming transfers altogether.
- **`+121` Live GPU-Resident MoE Experts (`3,813 -> 3,934`) at `[355, 356]` MiB Free VRAM**:
  - In-place HC expansion (`AFF_HC_INPLACE=1`), v2 dense arena aliasing (`AFF_DENSE_ARENA_ALIAS_V2=1`), lazy single-token arena (`AFF_SKIP_SINGLE_ARENA=1`), cross-module MoE/dense aliasing (`AFF_MOE_CROSS_ALIAS=1`), `VirtualSpan` lazy indexer E4M3 commits (`AFF_IDX_VMEM=1`), TP=2 row-sharded `engram_wkv` (`AFF_ENGRAM_TP_SHARD=1`), and TP=2 col-sharded `mtp_main_proj` (`AFF_MTP_PROJ_TP_SHARD=1`) reduce `dense weights` (`-144 MiB/card`), `draft dense` (`-38 MiB/card`), `prefill arena` (`908/910 -> 654/654 MiB`), and `compressor+indexer` (`218 -> 112 MiB/card`).
  - Reclaimed VRAM expands live GPU-resident routed MoE capacity from `3,813` to **`3,934` experts** (`3,864` shared = `25.2%` symmetric base + `70` Card-1 bonus shards / `425.5 MiB`), **`+216`** over `v5.7.0` and **`+121`** over `v5.8.0`.
- **`code_edit` Regression Reversed (`45.05 -> 46.43 tok/s`, Beating `v5.7.0` `45.54` by `+1.95%`)**:
  - `AFF_SUFFIX_GATE_V2=1` fixes the post-edit-boundary `SuffixGate` deadlock (`last_rejected` anchor guard + `proven_verbatim_relock` + `-20 ms` DSpark bypass credit), while Wave 5b ablation proved `sfx_burst_max=15` degraded 25%-resident PCIe MoE verify latency (`233 ms/blk`, `86.4%` acc); capping default `sfx_burst_max` to `sfx_max` (`12`) raised acceptance to **`95.1%` (`243` drafted, `231` accepted, `10.24 tok/blk`)** in **`25` blocks / `5.63 s` (`46.43 tok/s` `tg2`, `+3.06%` vs `v5.8.0`, `+1.95%` vs `v5.7.0`)**.
- **Prefill, DSpark & New `l64k pp2` Record (`2,716.2 tok/s`)**:
  - Wave 4 fusions (`AFF_FLASH_4WAVE_RESOLVE=1`, `AFF_FUSE_BATCH_KV_NORM_PREFILL=1`, `AFF_PREFILL_FUSE_HC_Q8=1`, `AFF_PREFILL_FUSE_AR_HC=1`) + `AFF_MARKOV_PERSIST=1` lift **`step_c` 4-prompt DSpark to `23.16 tok/s` (`+3.30%`)**, **`l16k tg1` to `46.96 tok/s` (`+9.18%`)**, **`p4k tg2` to `17.81 tok/s` (`+5.63%`)**, and push every prefill depth upward (`l4k pp1/pp2` `+1.81% / +4.23%`, **`l64k pp2` `2,660.0 -> 2,716.2 tok/s` `+2.11%`**).
- **Empirical `gfx1201` Cache-Hint & Kernel Ablation (4 Wave-3 Toggles Locked Opt-In OFF)**:
  - 4-arm ablation isolated both a `req00` (`0/9`) speculation failure and a `-6.2%..-10.5%` regression to four Wave-3 toggles (`AFF_GFX12_CACHE_HINTS`, `AFF_FP8_GEMM_V2`, `AFF_EXPERT_DECODE_V2`, `AFF_TP_AR_FIXED_TOK`); locking all four opt-in `0` restored `req00` (`2/2 ×4`, `0.18–0.25 s`) while retaining all beneficial Wave 1–4 gains.

## 2. Dual `gfx1201` Data-Transfer & Compression Audit

1. **PCIe Gen3 ×16 Host-to-Device (UVA MoE Expert Streaming)**:
   - Model container weights (`model-moonmath-1017.aff`, 2.875-bpw FP4/FP8) exhibit `7.996 bits/byte` Shannon entropy and `0.41%` zeros; `zlib -1` expands size (`1.0003×`). Lossless wire compression is negative-EV over direct UVA reads.
   - Only increasing static VRAM residency (`3,813 -> 3,934` experts) reduces PCIe H2D traffic per step without accuracy loss.
2. **Intra-Card GDDR6 & LDS Hierarchy (`gfx1201`, Wave32)**:
   - Prefill stages benefits from coalescing KV norm commits into `batch_rms_norm_rope_kernel` (`AFF_FUSE_BATCH_KV_NORM_PREFILL=1`), fusing HC collapse + E4M3 quantization (`AFF_PREFILL_FUSE_HC_Q8=1`), and expanding flash `Q` resolution to 4 waves (`AFF_FLASH_4WAVE_RESOLVE=1`).
   - Conversely, forcing unaligned `buffer_load_b96` + `TH_LOAD_NT_HT` (`AFF_EXPERT_DECODE_V2`), `kPingPong` on `NT=1` FP8 GEMM (`AFF_FP8_GEMM_V2`), scalar BAR writes (`AFF_TP_AR_FIXED_TOK`), or aggressive non-temporal `V#` hints (`AFF_GFX12_CACHE_HINTS`) regressed decode and warmup speculation, matching the `v5.8.0` finding that reusable dense/GEMV paths rely on `gfx1201`'s 8 MiB L2 + 64 MiB Infinity Cache MALL.
3. **Host/Peer Inter-GPU Path**:
   - Row-sharding `engram_wkv` (`AFF_ENGRAM_TP_SHARD=1`) and column-sharding `mtp_main_proj` (`AFF_MTP_PROJ_TP_SHARD=1`) halve dense replication across GPUs and exchange compact activations asynchronously; `rc3` fixes final-tile `ev_eng_done` peer synchronization so rank `d` cannot overwrite `D.e_out` (aliased onto `D.b_q`) before peers finish copying.
