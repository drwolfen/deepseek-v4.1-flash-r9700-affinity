## 1. Executive Summary

`v5.7.0` (`b223f20`, Wave 10B / Wave 2) targets DSpark Markov/draft bottlenecks, remaining FP8 E4M3 conversion paths, and PCIe round trips (`W2-1` through `W2-9`) across dual `gfx1201` R9700 cards while keeping `-ffp-contract=off` (`33/33`), RDNA 4 zero-scratch/zero-VGPR-spill guarantees, and prefix-cache compatibility (`0x733de762fee543e6`) intact:

- **+19 Resident GPU MoE Experts (`3,699 -> 3,718`) at `[355, 352]` MiB Free VRAM**:
  - Mapping `mtp_markov_w1` via UVA host RAM (`W2-2`: `63.1 MiB` pinned host, `0 MiB` VRAM/card) and row-splitting `mtp_markov_w2` as group-128 FP8 E4M3 (`W2-1`: `16.0 MiB/shard` vs `31.6 MiB/shard` BF16) shrinks DSpark dense weights from `474 / 468 MiB` (`5.48 GiB` total) in `v5.6.2` down to **`362 / 356 MiB` (`5.22 GiB` total)** and expands live GPU-resident routed MoE experts to **`3,718`** (`3,622` shared + `96` Card-1 asymmetric bonus shards / `583.5 MiB`, `+74` vs `v5.6.1`, `+125` vs `v5.4.0` live).
- **New `code_edit` & `l4k` Decode Peaks, Consistent Prefill & `l64k` Gains**:
  - Final clean `v5.7.0` (`b223f20`) reaches **`45.54 tok/s` `code_edit tg2`** (**`+6.18%`** vs paired `arm2_v562` `42.89`, **`+4.09%`** vs clean `v5.6.2` `43.75`), **`50.57 tok/s` `l4k tg2`** (**`+3.54%`** vs `arm2_v562` `48.84`; `53.79 tok/s` / `+10.14%` in Phase 2B `arm3a_ar_bf16_0`), **`50.17 tok/s` `l64k tg2`** (`+2.14%`), **`1,412.3 / 2,594.3 tok/s` `l64k pp1/pp2`** (`+2.42%` / `+2.57%`), **`1,302.2 tok/s` `l16k pp1`** (`46.73` cold `tg1`, `+5.51%`), and **`148.78 ms/blk` (`144.09 ms/blk` in `arm3a_ar_bf16_0`)** on `step_c` 4-prompt DSpark.
- **Empirical Ablation Locked `AFF_AR_HC_BF16=0` Opt-In (`W2-5`)**:
  - Phase 2 testing on `v5.7.0-rc1` proved that casting target-layer `tp_ar_batch_hc_expand` partial sums to BF16 before TP=2 summation (`AFF_AR_HC_BF16=1`) degraded speculative warmup (`req00 2/2 -> 0/3`, `0.25 s -> 0.46 s`) and regressed `step_c` (`149.22 -> 168.52 ms/blk`, `21.62 -> 20.30 tok/s`). Setting `AFF_AR_HC_BF16=0` default in `arm3a_ar_bf16_0` and `b223f20` immediately restored `req00 2/2` (`0.25–0.27 s`) and accelerated `step_c` to `144.09 ms/blk` / `22.13 tok/s` (`+2.36%` over `arm2_v562`).
- **Full Audit & Verification Ledger**:
  - Passes `ldd` (`0` missing), **`33/33` `-ffp-contract=off`**, **`12/12` offline CPU `ctest`** (`0.44 s`), AMDGPU ELF audit across all 3 binaries (`706` / `705` / `701` kernels with **`0` scratch / `0` VGPR spill / `0` dynamic stack** and `flash_kernel<2>` reduced `235 -> 230` VGPRs at `occ=6`), **`5/5` GPU suites** (including `test_gpu_ports` Tests 29–31 and `test_wave2_kernels` Tests 15–18) + `2/2` RC1 toggle-OFF fallback checks, `DRY 3/3`, `test_a_stride` (`33,792/35,144`), `test_b_think_ckpt` (`2,500/2,618`), `probe_bug567` `all_pass=true`, `N0 = 100.0%` (`47,804,160 / 47,804,160`), `0` `hipError`, `3.565 s` KFD release, and live `:8000` cutover smoke `'391'`.

### 2.1 `W2-5` (`AFF_AR_HC_BF16`) Ablation Finding

On `v5.7.0-rc1` (`arm1_v570_rc1`, `AFF_AR_HC_BF16=1`), rounding TP=2 partial sums to 7-bit BF16 mantissa on the peer wire across 80 target residual sublayers introduced cumulative numerical jitter ahead of TP=2 addition:

- Warmup speculative probe `req00` dropped from `2/2` (`100.0%` accepted, `0.25 s`) to `0/3` (`0.0%` accepted, `0.46 s`).
- 4-prompt deterministic `step_c` regressed from `21.62 tok/s` (`149.22 ms/blk`) on `arm2_v562` to `20.30 tok/s` (`168.52 ms/blk`, `+12.93%` per-block latency).

Disabling only `AFF_AR_HC_BF16=0` on the identical RC1 binary (`arm3a_ar_bf16_0`) immediately restored `req00` to `2/2` (`0.25 s`), improved `step_c` to **`22.13 tok/s` (`144.09 ms/blk`)**, and lifted `l4k tg2` to **`53.79 tok/s` (`+10.14%`)**. `W2-5` was therefore kept compiled and unit-tested (`test_gpu_ports` Test 29) as an opt-in knob (`AFF_AR_HC_BF16=0` default) in `b223f20`.
