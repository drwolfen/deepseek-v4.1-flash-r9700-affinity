## 1. Executive Summary

- **100% Deterministic FAST Regime (`6/6` runs vs bimodal `v5.3.0`)**: Auto-setting `GPU_MAX_HW_QUEUES=1` on `gfx12xx` paired with a single-SE 25% contiguous hybrid CU mask (`AFF_HYBRID_CU_FRAC=0.25`, `AFF_HYBRID_CU_STRIPE=0` -> `8/32 WGPs`, `0x000000ff`, `10` KFD queues total vs `16` in `v5.3.0`) eliminates the ROCm/TheRock `#9297` cross-queue wait penalty (`~17–26 us` per small kernel launch) and multi-SE CU-mask contention that caused `v5.3.0` to flip randomly between `28–33 tok/s` and `41–45 tok/s`.
- **Warm Decode (`45.81–48.24 tok/s`, `+6.16%` to `+10.13%` over FAST `v5.3.0`)**: On `bench_pp_tg.py` (`NGEN=192`), `v5.4.0-rc5` beats BOTH paired `v5.3.0` (`rc5_arm1`) and prior FAST `v5.3.0` (`rc4_arm1_fast`):
  - `4K` (`4,031` tok): `pp1 = 624.0 tok/s` (`+3.28%` / `+2.94%`), `tg2 = 48.24 tok/s` (`+8.16%` / `+6.16%`).
  - `16K` (`16,104` tok): `pp1 = 1,300.5 tok/s` (`+7.37%` / `+7.26%`), `tg2 = 45.81 tok/s` (`+1.15%` / `+3.92%`).
  - `64K` (`63,863` tok): `pp1 = 1,409.0 tok/s` (`45.3 s`, `+4.83%` / `+4.27%`), `tg2 = 46.73 tok/s` (`+17.86%` / `+10.13%`).
- **4-Prompt `temp=0.0` DSpark (`22.70 tok/s` mean, `+15.17%` / `+9.08%`)**: Every prompt accelerates (`math_proof 22.27`, `cpp_bug 21.84`, `json_schema 26.75`, `logic_puzzle 19.94 tok/s`).
- **Multi-Turn `80K -> 123K` Context (`77.70 s` cold TTFT, `0.90–0.95 s` cached TTFT)**: Cold `80K` TTFT drops `-4.38%` / `-4.79%`, `80K -> 123K` extension drops `-4.59%` / `-2.89%` (`37.03 s`), and cached turns resume `80,393` and `123,374` tokens in `0.949 s` and `0.901 s`.

## 3. RDNA 4 (`gfx1201`) KFD Queue & CU-Mask Root-Cause Ablation

Subtracting `dense-gpu` and `routed-expert` `AFF_TRACE=dense,events,deep` traces revealed an exact **`+17 to +26 us` per kernel launch** across every GPU op (`x_swiglu 41 us vs 16 us`, `b_attend 104 us vs 40 us`, `b_hc_mv 45 us vs 13.5 us`, `b_qkv 401 us vs 182 us`) matching ROCm/TheRock `#9297` combined with KFD CWSR pipe scheduling (`num_cp_queues = 4` = 2 pipes × 2 HQDs) and multi-SE WGP mask contention.

1. Leaving `GPU_MAX_HW_QUEUES=4` (`m4` vs `m9`, `m1`, and `v5.3.0` paired runs) allocates `16` KFD queues and randomly hits FAST or SLOW (~50/50) depending on queue-to-CP-pipe mapping.

3. Assigning the CU-masked hybrid stream strictly to a **single Shader Engine** (`0.25` contiguous = `0x000000ff`, SE0 only, `8/32 WGPs`, `10` KFD queues) avoids the 16-WGP multi-SE stall seen in `m2`/`m3`/`m6` (`0x0000ffff` SE0+SE1 and `0x0f0f0f0f` all-SEs), keeps `SE1..SE3` uncontended for resident GEMMs, and achieves **`44.7–48.2 tok/s`** (`6/6` FAST) with `0.00 GiB` D2H.

## 6. Agentic Client Best Practices (Hermes / Tool-Calling Harnesses)

Production trace analysis of long agentic sessions identified these client-side behaviors that materially affect TTFT and cache hit rates:
1. **Align client `cache_ttl` with local execution timeouts**: Setting `cache_ttl: 5m` ensures periodic keep-warm/prefix alignment across long local bash/tool pauses.
2. **Offload context compression**: Routing auxiliary context summarization away from the busy `:8000` single-slot engine prevents blocking interactive coding turns during compaction.
3. **Avoid mutating assistant history across turns**: Preserve raw `reasoning` (without duplicating `reasoning_content` + `reasoning` with divergent formatting) and preserve JSON argument key order inside historical `tool_calls` so Affinity's prefix cache resumes from the `</think>` / post-turn checkpoint cleanly (while Affinity's new `</think>` checkpoint still rescues up to `</think>` when callers reorder `tool_calls` keys).
