## Executive Summary

Wave 14 (`v5.11.0`) investigated four architectural questions on the dual-`gfx1201` platform:

1. **Why GPU board power averages ~130–155 W (max ~180–198 W) below the 210 W TBP limit while 256 AI Accelerators are present:**  
   Continuous 1 Hz telemetry across paired FAST runs and the full `v5.11.0` verification gate confirms GPU active occupancy is already **95.4%–97.4%** (`96.9% / 96.8%` in `final_v511`), while GPU0/GPU1 draw **`141.4 / 152.6 W` avg (`187 / 198 W` max)** in `final_v511` (`132.5 / 143.5 W` in `rc1_default` vs `128.5 / 136.7 W` in paired `ctrl_v510`). The headroom below 210 W comes from **PCIe Gen3 ×16 hybrid MoE streaming latency** across layers rather than under-dispatched compute: aggressive preloading (`AFF_DSPARK_PRELOAD=1`) saturated PCIe contention, spiked GPU0 instantaneous power to **`329 W`**, and regressed `l16k tg2` from **`55.89 -> 36.94 tok/s`** (`-33.9%`). The durable path to higher accelerator feeding is higher VRAM residency plus better L0/L1/L2/64 MiB MALL cache discipline.
2. **Dual-GPU (`TP=2`) VRAM layout symmetry & 2 MiB dense weight slab packing (`facc47f`):**  
   A best-fit **2 MiB dense weight slab allocator** (`AFF_DENSE_WEIGHT_SLAB=1`), 2 MiB KV/SplitK trim (`346 -> 338 MiB/card`), and **auto-rank router ownership rebalance** (`AFF_ROUTER_RANK=auto`, selecting Rank 1 because `router_rank0_only` already puts replicated dense headroom on Rank 0) reclaim **`-290 MiB` on GPU0 (`4,252 -> 3,962 MiB` dense)** and achieve symmetric **`3,948` GPU-resident routed experts** (**`3,938` shared + `10` bonus** vs `3,888 + 58` in `v5.10.0`).
3. **Portable multi-ISA CPU SIMD dispatch (`6f7428c`):**  
   Replaced compile-time AVX512/AVX2 host assumptions with runtime CPUID/OSXSAVE dispatch (`AFF_CPU_ISA=scalar|avx1|avx2|avx512|avx512vbmi`) across `Scalar`, `Avx1`, `Avx2` (+FMA/F16C), `Avx512`, and `Avx512Vbmi`, defaulting builds to **`AFF_CPU_ARCH=avx`** (portable to SandyBridge+ AVX1 CPUs while still dispatching AVX-512/VBMI at full speed on Skylake-SP/Zen4+ at runtime) plus **`AFF_CPU_ARCH=portable`** (SSE2 baseline for `qemu64`+ CPUs). Verified with **0** static ISA violations (`audit_cpu_isa.py`) and clean execution across QEMU `SandyBridge`, `Haswell`, `Skylake-Server`, `Icelake-Server`, and `qemu64` (portable).
4. **`gfx1201` Cache Hierarchy Tuning (`2f20816`) & AI Accelerator WMMA Experiments (`09827f3`):**  
   Decoupled `AFF_LMHEAD_NT=1`, fixed FP8 GEMM tail prefetch (`prefetch_load_nt` guarding out-of-bounds K tails), parameterized `AFF_GEMM_SWIZZLE=8` CTA M-band swizzle across `MTW={1,2}` for 64 MiB MALL reuse, and added thresholded non-temporal paths `AFF_IDX_SCORE_NT_STORE=1` (`>= 4 MiB`) and `AFF_FLASH_LONGCTX_NT=1` (`>= 32 MiB`, with inline `asm("global_load_tr_b64 ... th:TH_LOAD_NT")` deliberately rejected after disassembly revealed LLVM `SIInsertWaitcnts` omitted `s_wait_loadcnt` before `v_wmma`). Experimental AI Accelerator WMMA kernels (`AFF_LMHEAD_WMMA=0`, `AFF_DRAFT_KV_FP8_WMMA=0`) and DSpark expert preload (`AFF_DSPARK_PRELOAD=0`) are kept opt-in after 5-arm FAST ablation showed default `v5.11.0` outperforms them on end-to-end throughput.
5. **Forced Think-Close Prefix-Cache BPE Boundary Fix (`457d252`):**  
   Removed the leading `\n` from `ThinkingBudget`'s forced-close sequence (`</think>\n\n` instead of `\n</think>\n\n`), preventing Turn-2 BPE merges (e.g. `.\n` token `603`) and aligning forced-close prefix-cache checkpoints (`st.pos` before `</think>`) with natural closes so multi-turn `test_b_think_ckpt` restores reliably even when Turn 1 hits `hard_limit`.

**Takeaways:**
- **`AFF_DSPARK_PRELOAD=1`** verifies why "MTP for experts" hurts hybrid MoE: preloading DSpark hash-predicted experts consumes host-to-device PCIe Gen3 ×16 DMA bandwidth directly in contention with synchronous `expert_gemm_wmma_kernel` UVA reads, causing `329 W` instantaneous power spikes and collapsing `l16k tg2` by `-33.9%`.
- **`AFF_LMHEAD_WMMA=1`** adds BF16->FP8 activation staging and reduction overhead on narrow `T = 2..16` vocabulary GEMVs (`129,280 × 5,120`), which are memory-bandwidth bound rather than compute-bound on `gfx1201`. `AFF_LMHEAD_FP8=1` scalarized GEMV (`matvec_fp8_g128_multix`) remains significantly faster (`51.05` vs `46.32` `l4k`, `55.89` vs `45.10` `l16k`).
- **`AFF_DRAFT_KV_FP8_WMMA=1`** boosts `l16k pp1` (`1,346.4`) and `l16k tg2` (`57.79`) with near-identical `step_c` (`24.39`, `127.90 ms/blk`), so it is retained as a clean opt-in flag while keeping the conservative `rc1_default` (`0`) that won across `tg1` (`58.88`) and `step_c` (`24.45 / 127.80 ms/blk`).

## 3. Component Deep Dive

### 3.1 Dual-GPU Slab Allocation & Symmetry (`facc47f`)
- **2 MiB Dense Weight Slab (`AFF_DENSE_WEIGHT_SLAB=1`, `src/gpu/dense_gpu.hip`):** Groups dense weights into shared `2 MiB` HIP allocations using best-fit packing (excluding `>16 MiB` exact-granularity allocations), cutting small-BO HIP/KFD virtual allocation fragmentation overhead (`dense_vram` `4,252 -> 3,962 MiB` on GPU0).
- **2 MiB KV/SplitK Trim (`src/gpu/dense_gpu.hip`):** Tightens slack alignment in KV/SplitK arenas (`kv cache` `346 -> 338 MiB/card`).
- **Auto-Rank Router Balance (`AFF_ROUTER_RANK=auto`, `src/gpu/dense_gpu.{h,hip}`, `tests/test_gpu_ports.hip`):** When `AFF_ROUTER_RANK0_ONLY=1` eliminates replicated router gates on one GPU, `AFF_ROUTER_RANK=auto` automatically assigns router ownership to Rank 1 where free VRAM / dense asymmetry is smaller, equalizing headrooms and raising shared experts `3,888 -> 3,938` (`+50` shared, `3,948` total).

### 3.2 Portable Multi-ISA CPU SIMD Dispatch (`6f7428c`)
- **Problem:** Prior releases compiled C++ translation units and `libaff_rust_accel.a` with `-march=native` (`-mavx512*`), causing `SIGILL` on AVX1/AVX2-only machines (e.g., SandyBridge, Haswell, older EPYC/Xeon without AVX-512).
- **Solution (`src/engine/cpu_dispatch.{h,cpp}`, `CMakeLists.txt`, `Dockerfiles`):**
  - Build-time `AFF_CPU_ARCH` supports `portable` (`-march=x86-64` SSE2), `avx` (**new default**, `-mavx -mno-avx2 -mno-fma -mno-f16c -mno-avx512f`), and `native` (`-march=native`). `AFF_NATIVE` is preserved as a deprecated alias for `native`.
  - Runtime CPUID + XGETBV (`hw_max_cpu_isa()`, overridable via `AFF_CPU_ISA=scalar|avx1|avx2|avx512|avx512vbmi`) dynamically selects specialized `__attribute__((target(...)))` kernels across `engram.cpp` (`gather_mxfp8`), `expert_kernel.cpp`, `matvec_bf16.inc`, `matvec_fp8.inc`, `model.cpp`, `blockquant.cpp`, `server.cpp` (`scan_clean_ascii_avx2`), `ops.cpp`, and `vit.cpp`, plus matching runtime target features in `src/rust_accel/lib.rs` (when compiled statically with baseline `+avx` or `x86-64`).
  - Audited via `audit_cpu_isa.py` (`0` leaked higher-ISA instructions in baseline/Rust objects for both `--mode avx` and `--mode portable`) and verified on QEMU (`SandyBridge`, `Haswell`, `Skylake-Server`, `Icelake-Server` on `avx`, and all 5 including `qemu64` on `portable`).

### 3.3 `gfx1201` Cache Hierarchy & Compiler Hazard Audit (`2f20816`)
- **`AFF_LMHEAD_NT=1` (`src/gpu/batch_kernels.hip`):** Separates LM-head weight non-temporal streaming (`TH_LOAD_NT`) from `AFF_DENSE_NT_LOAD=0` so large vocab matrices bypass the 64 MiB MALL without evicting hot layers.
- **FP8 GEMM Tail Prefetch Fix (`src/gpu/batch_kernels.hip`):** Guards `prefetch_load_nt` at loop tail to prevent illegal/wasted prefetch reads beyond K bounds.
- **Extended CTA Swizzle (`AFF_GEMM_SWIZZLE=8`, `src/gpu/batch_kernels.hip`):** Generalizes `group_m` M-band CTA grouping across both `MTW=1` and `MTW=2` tiles for improved 64 MiB MALL weight reuse.
- **Non-Temporal Thresholds on Large Indexer/Flash Spans:** `AFF_IDX_SCORE_NT_STORE=1` (`AFF_IDX_SCORE_NT_BYTES=4194304`) uses non-temporal global stores for large indexer score matrices (`>= 4 MiB`), and `AFF_FLASH_LONGCTX_NT=1` (`AFF_FLASH_NT_MIB=32`) enables `NT_KV` streaming hints on K and row scales once KV exceeds 32 MiB (`batch_kernels.hip`, `indexer_gpu.hip`, `attn_flash.hip`).
- **Disassembly Finding (`task-5818`, `src/gpu/attn_flash.hip`):** Inspecting compiled `gfx1201` GCN ISA showed raw inline `asm("global_load_tr_b64 ... th:TH_LOAD_NT")` is opaque to LLVM's `SIInsertWaitcnts` hazard pass, omitting `s_wait_loadcnt` before `v_wmma` (a silent correctness hazard caught before GPU testing). V-tile transposed loads therefore remain on `__builtin_amdgcn_global_load_tr_b64_v2i32`, while `NT_KV` applies safely to K and row scales.

### 3.4 Forced Think-Close Prefix-Cache BPE Boundary Fix (`457d252`)
- **Symptom:** During Attempt 1 of `final_v511`, `test_b_think_ckpt` reasoned to `1024` tokens, triggered `ThinkingBudget::force_close("hard_limit")`, published `think-close checkpoint at 1480 tokens`, and restored `0/1595` tokens on Turn 2 (`2 blocks of 3 matched`, checkpoint miss).
- **Root Cause:** Injecting `\n</think>\n\n` (`{201, 128822, 271}`) appended `201` (`\n`) before `128822` (`</think>`), which merged with preceding punctuation/whitespace under Turn-2 BPE (e.g. `.` + `\n` -> `603` `".\n"`), while `(!forcing || i == k)` deferred checkpoint publication to `1480` (post-`\n\n`) instead of pre-`</think>`.
- **Fix:** Initialize `think_close_ids` from `</think>\n\n` (`{128822, 271}`) in [`tools/affinity.cpp`](../tools/affinity.cpp#L1097). Because `</think>` (`128822`) is an `added_token` tokenized before BPE, it never merges with preceding text and fires the checkpoint immediately before itself (`st.pos = prompt + thought_tokens`), while `{271}` (`\n\n`) is injected on the next forcing step. Covered by `test_forced_close_prefix_cache_bpe_invariance()` in [`tests/test_thinking_budget.cpp`](../tests/test_thinking_budget.cpp).
