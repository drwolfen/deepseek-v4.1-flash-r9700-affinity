## 1. Executive Summary

`v5.3.0` delivers Wave A and Wave B RDNA 4 (`gfx1201`, Wave32) HIP kernel optimizations plus the post-Wave B multi-GEMM contiguous output-band and WGP L0 acquire-fence fix (`e1f6be3`), verified across a clean 3-arm paired benchmark with zero functional regressions:

- **Decode & Warm Generation (`+24.9%` to `+43.8%` `tg`)**: Paired `bench_pp_tg.py` (`NGEN=192`) on ROCm `10.1.0-full` improves cold Pass-1 decode (`tg1`) to `25.70–41.88 tok/s` (**`+27.2%` to `+43.8%`** over `v5.2.0`) and warm Pass-2 decode (`tg2`) to `41.68–42.23 tok/s` (**`+24.9%` to `+37.4%`** over `v5.2.0`).
- **Slot Throughput (`+37.05%` `blocks/s`)**: Mean slot step throughput across all paired server runs increases from `5.586` to **`7.656 blocks/s`** (`+37.05%`).
- **4-Prompt `temp=0.0` DSpark (`18.59–24.95 tok/s`)**: Mean streaming decode rises from `15.81 tok/s` to **`21.33 tok/s`** (`+34.9%`), with coherent outputs and full acceptance (`66.8%–98.5%`, `2.75–4.74 tok/blk`).
- **Multi-Turn `64K -> 100K` Cached Decode (`+20.2%` to `+49.0%` `tg`)**: Cached Turn 2 reaches `23.89 tok/s` (`+49.0%`, TTFT `1.056 s`), Turn 3 extension reaches `26.30 tok/s` (`+33.0%`, TTFT `39.282 s`), and cached Turn 4 reaches `20.08 tok/s` (`+42.2%`, TTFT `1.002 s`).
- **CLI `AFF_TRACE` Kernel Profile**: `dense-gpu` device time drops **`-34.0%`** (`9.943 s -> 6.560 s`), `routed-expert` device time drops **`-23.0%` / `-23.7%`**, `x_gu + x_down` drops **`-85.2%`**, `b_reduce` drops **`-46.5%`**, `reduce` drops **`-31.8%`**, `b_attend` drops **`-27.6%`**, and `b_gate` drops **`-11.8%`**.
- **Toolchain Gate (`+3.55%` ROCm 10.1 Advantage)**: ROCm `10.1.0-full` (`mean_pp=1017.35`, `mean_tg=39.19`, `mean_bps=7.656`, `mean_step_c=21.33`) outperforms ROCm `10.2.0-full` (`mean_pp=1021.97`, `mean_tg=38.84`, `mean_bps=7.379`, `mean_step_c=19.17`) by **`+3.55%` composite**, keeping `ROCm 10.1.0-full` as the default `Dockerfile` base image.
- **VRAM & KFD Stability**: Resident MoE capacity remains **`3,517` experts** at `free_vram_mib=[353, 355]` (`0` OOM, `0` spill) and clean KFD release in **`2.800 s`**.

## 2. Engineering Changes & ELF Audit (`dfb34cb`, `c20ed32`, `e1f6be3`)

### 2.1 Wave A: Zero-Functional-Change RDNA4 Kernel Optimizations (`dfb34cb`)
- Added shared RDNA4 Wave32 primitives in `src/gpu/hip_common.h`: `lds_barrier()` (local-only fence + `s_barrier` skipping global invalidation/drain), `dpp_xor_lane<Lane>()` (`DPP16 row_xmask` for offsets 1–8 and `v_permlanex16_b32` for offset 16, bypassing `ds_bpermute_b32`), and `AFF_WAVES(n)` occupancy hints.
- Optimized occupancy, LDS barriers, Wave32 reductions, and launch overhead across `attention_gpu.hip`, `attn_flash.hip`, `batch_kernels.hip`, `compressor_gpu.hip`, `dense_gpu.hip`, `device_math.h`, `engram_gpu.hip`, `expert_kernel.hip`, `indexer_gpu.hip`, `keepalive.hip`, `moe_plan.hip`, `sampler_gpu.hip`, `static_placement.hip`, `tp_allreduce.hip`, and `vmem.hip`.
- Expanded `tests/test_gpu_ports.hip` with kernel parity and boundary coverage.

### 2.2 Wave B: RDNA4 FP8 4x2 Prefill, Inline Split-K, Staged TP2 AR & VGPR/LDS Fixes (`c20ed32`)
- **FP8 Prefill 4×2 & Inline Split-K (`batch_kernels.hip`)**: Added `AFF_FP8_PREFILL_4X2` (`4×2` tile schedule for FP8 prefill GEMMs) and `AFF_FP8_SPLIT_INLINE` (inline split-K reduction avoiding separate finisher launches).
- **Staged TP2 All-Reduce (`tp_allreduce.hip`)**: Added `AFF_TP_AR_STAGED` for low-latency FP8 staged TP=2 all-reduce transfers.
- **Flash Attention & Indexer VGPR/LDS Tuning (`attn_flash.hip`, `indexer_gpu.hip`)**: Added `AFF_FLASH_MIN_TILES` scheduling guard and tightened `flash_kernel<2>` / `idx_quant_kernel` register and LDS footprints.
- Added `tests/test_wave2_kernels.hip` covering FP8 4×2 + inline split-K parity across shapes, `ps={0,1}`, `acc={0,1}`, and `rep={0,1}`.

### 2.3 Post-Wave B Multi-GEMM Band & WGP Fence Fix (`e1f6be3`)
- Fixed `batch_gemm_multi_hip` and `batch_gemm_fp8_multi_hip` in `src/gpu/batch_kernels.hip` so multi-GEMM jobs with `jobs[i].y == nullptr` (`i > 0`) index contiguous output bands relative to `jobs[0].y` rather than dereferencing null `jobs[i].y`.
- Replaced `__threadfence()` with `__builtin_amdgcn_fence(__ATOMIC_ACQUIRE, "agent")` under `__HIP_DEVICE_COMPILE__` in `gemm_fp8_body` for correct RDNA4 WGP L0 cache acquire semantics during inline split-K accumulation.
- Added `fp8_multi_null_band` (`1536+576`, `256+256` at `ps=0/1`) and `bf16_multi_null_band` (`256+128`) regressions to `tests/test_wave2_kernels.hip`.

### 2.4 Fatbin ELF Audit (`gfx1201`)
Audited all **`497`** `gfx1201` kernels in the compiled binary on both ROCm `10.1.0-full` and `10.2.0-full`:
- `flash_kernel<2u, ...>`: **`235` VGPRs** (ROCm 10.1) / **`240` VGPRs** (ROCm 10.2), **`21,392` B LDS**, `vgpr_spill=0`, `scratch=0`, occupancy **`occ=6`** waves/SIMD.
- `idx_quant_kernel` & `idx_quant128_kernel`: `11` VGPRs, `0` / `32` B LDS, **`scratch=0`**, `vgpr_spill=0`.
- `tp_ar_fp8_staged_kernel`: `21` VGPRs, `4` B LDS, **`scratch=0`**, `vgpr_spill=0`.
- **New spills**: **`0`** across all modified kernels (only pre-existing untouched `hcn_v41_collapse_sq_kernel<16u, 8u>` reports static scratch `292` B).
